One-time setup · about 1 minute

Get this phone ready

After setup, the tabulator opens like an app and keeps working even if the Wi-Fi drops. No internet is used.

1

Trust the tabulator

  1. Tap Download certificate.
  2. Open Settings › Security › More security settings › Install from device storage › CA certificate (the menu names differ a little by brand; search Settings for "CA certificate").
  3. Pick icpep-tabulator.crt and confirm.
  1. Open this page in Safari, tap Download certificate, then Allow.
  2. Open Settings › Profile Downloaded › Install.
  3. Then Settings › General › About › Certificate Trust Settings and turn on ICPEP Tabulator (shown as "Caddy Local Authority").
Download certificate
2

Check the secure connection

  1. Tap Check now. When it says ready, tap Open the app.
Open the app
3

Add it to your Home screen

  1. In the app, tap Chrome's ⋮ menu › Add to Home screen › Install.
  2. From now on, open the Tabulator icon.
  1. In the app, tap Share › Add to Home Screen › Add.
  2. From now on, open the Tabulator icon.

Skipping setup still works: open and keep the page open. It just can't reopen while the Wi-Fi is down.
